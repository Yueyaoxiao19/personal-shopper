# The question bank

This is a bank, not a sequence. Nine of these are the opening brief in SKILL.md.
Stage 2b is the category kit, which runs before the first pull. A handful fire on the
triggers in Step 4, and the rest are here for someone who says yes at the check-in and
wants to go deeper.

**Core** means ask of everyone. Everything else appears only when an earlier answer
makes it relevant. Never ask a man's inseam of someone shopping women's dresses, and
never ask about necklines when someone is buying gloves.

## Stage 1, getting started

| Question | Options | Shown when |
|---|---|---|
| **Core.** What brings you shopping today? | An event or occasion · A new chapter · A specific item in mind · Not sure yet, help me find my vibe | Always |
| Tell me about the item you have in mind | Typed | Specific item. Starts the fast lane |
| **Core.** Which section should I shop in? | Women's · Men's · Gender neutral | Always, multi |
| **Core.** What would you like me to find? | Clothing · Activewear · Shoes and socks · Scarves, hats, gloves | Always, multi |
| A complete outfit, or just a few pieces? | A complete outfit · Just a few pieces | Clothing selected |
| Which pieces? | Women's: Tops · Bottoms · Dresses or jumpsuits · Outerwear. Men's: Tops · Bottoms · Suits and blazers · Outerwear | A few pieces, multi |
| **Core.** How many pieces today? | Just one · Two or three · Four or five · A full refresh | Always |
| Which of these would you love to add? | Scarves · Hats or beanies · Gloves | Scarves/hats/gloves selected |

## Stage 2, occasion and context

Run only the branch the first answer points at. Activewear runs alongside whichever applies.

### Event

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| What's the occasion? | A wedding or formal celebration · A work event or interview · A date or night out · A trip or vacation |
| Is there a dress code? | Casual · Smart casual · Cocktail or semi-formal · Black tie or formal |
| What will the weather be like? | Hot and sunny · Mild and pleasant · Cool · Cold |
| Indoors, outdoors, or both? | Mostly indoors · Mostly outdoors · A bit of both |

### New chapter

Ask which first: a new job · a change of season · an everyday refresh.

**A new job**

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| Dress code at the new workplace? | Business formal · Business casual · Casual · Not sure yet |
| What kind of workplace? *(only if not sure)* | Corporate like finance or law · Creative or tech · Healthcare or hands-on · Retail or customer-facing |
| What are you hoping to put together? | A great first-day outfit · A few key pieces · A starter work wardrobe I can mix and match |
| How many days a week in person? | One or two · Three or four · Every day · Remote, mostly video calls |

**A change of season**

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| Which season? | Spring · Summer · Fall · Winter |
| What's it like where you live? | Hot and humid · Warm and dry · Cool and rainy · Cold and snowy |
| Pieces that work across seasons too? | Yes, layering please · No, just this season |

**An everyday refresh**

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| What does a typical week look like? | Office or in-person · Working from home · Errands and casual plans · Workouts or active days (multi) |

### Activewear

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| Which pieces? | Tops or sports bras · Leggings, shorts or joggers · Jackets or layers · Socks (multi) |
| What kind of movement? | Pilates or barre · Yoga · Gym and strength · Running or cardio (multi) |
| What matters most? | Sweat-wicking and breathable · Strong support · Pockets · Stays put when I move (multi) |
| How much support? *(tops or bras)* | Light · Medium · High |
| Grip socks? *(socks)* | Yes please · Regular athletic · Not sure |

## Stage 2b, the category kit

Ask these before the first pull, never at the check-in. They decide which variant of a
garment exists, which is why they cannot wait for a reaction. Run only the kit for the
category they chose, and skip anything the brief already answered.

### Bottoms

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** How tall are you? | Under 5'3" · 5'3" to 5'6" · 5'6" to 5'9" · 5'9" and up |
| **Core.** Where should they sit at the waist? | High · Mid · Low · No preference |
| **Core.** How wide through the leg? | Slim or tapered · Straight · Wide · Barrel or baggy |
| **Core.** Waist style? | Zip and button · Elastic or pull-on · Either is fine |
| Where should the hem land? | Cropped at the ankle · Just touching the shoe · Longer, breaking on the shoe |
| Jeans only: how much give? | Rigid denim · A little stretch · Very stretchy |

### Sweaters and knits

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** Necklines you'd rather skip? | Turtleneck · Mock or funnel · Deep V · Boat or off-shoulder · Happy with all (multi) |
| **Core.** How should it fit? | Fitted · True to size · Relaxed · Oversized |
| **Core.** Where should it end? | Cropped · At the waistband · Over the hip · Longer |
| **Core.** How warm does it need to be? | Lightweight, for layering · Midweight · Chunky and warm |
| Over a collar, or on its own? | Over a shirt · On its own · Both |

### Tops and shirts

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** Necklines you'd rather skip? | Turtleneck · Deep V · Halter or off-shoulder · Happy with all (multi) |
| **Core.** Sleeve length? | Long · Three-quarter · Short · Sleeveless is fine (multi) |
| **Core.** How should it fit? | Fitted · True to size · Relaxed · Oversized |
| Anything you'd rather not show? | Arms · Shoulders · Neckline or chest · Midriff · Nothing in particular (multi) |
| Tucked or untucked? | Tucked · Untucked · Either |

### Outerwear

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** How tall are you? | Under 5'3" · 5'3" to 5'6" · 5'6" to 5'9" · 5'9" and up |
| **Core.** How long should it be? | Cropped · Hip length · Mid-thigh · Knee or longer |
| **Core.** What goes under it? | A shirt · A sweater · A thick sweater or two layers |
| **Core.** Which city will you wear it in? | Typed. Climate only, never an address |
| Hood? | Yes · No · Don't mind |
| How water resistant? | Doesn't matter · Light rain · Properly waterproof |

### Dresses

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** How tall are you? | Under 5'3" · 5'3" to 5'6" · 5'6" to 5'9" · 5'9" and up |
| **Core.** How long? | Mini · Knee · Midi · Maxi (multi) |
| **Core.** Sleeves? | Long · Short · Sleeveless is fine · Strapless is fine (multi) |
| **Core.** Necklines you'd rather skip? | Turtleneck · Plunging · Strapless or off-shoulder · Happy with all (multi) |
| Anything you'd rather not show? | Arms · Shoulders · Back · Legs · Nothing in particular (multi) |

### Shoes

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** Size, and any width needs? | Typed |
| **Core.** Heel height? | Flat · Low · Mid · High |
| **Core.** Where will you walk in them? | Office floors · City pavement · Snow and salt · Mostly seated |

**Never ask for weight, and never ask which body parts to highlight or minimise.** You
cannot see them, and the useful version of that question is "anything you'd rather not
show", which maps cleanly onto necklines, sleeves and hems.

## Stage 3, style and vibe

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** How would you love to feel in what you're wearing? | Confident and powerful · Comfortable and relaxed · Polished and elegant · Playful and fun (multi) |
| **Core.** Which style feels most like you? | Classic and timeless · Minimal and clean · Relaxed and casual · Bold and trendy (multi) |
| How adventurous should my picks be? | Close to what I know · A little outside my comfort zone · Surprise me |
| **Core.** Which colours make you happiest? | Neutrals · Earthy tones · Soft pastels · Bright or jewel tones (multi) |
| **Core.** Any colours to avoid? | None · Bright or neon · Pastels · Earthy tones (multi) |
| **Core.** How do you feel about prints? | Solids only · Subtle patterns · Bold prints · Anything goes |

These three are promoted: they run alongside the Stage 2b kit, before the first pull,
whatever the category. The rest of Stage 3 still waits for the check-in.

### Vibe discovery

Replaces the two Core questions above when someone picked "not sure yet, help me find
my vibe". Indirect questions get at taste far better than asking someone to name their
own style, which almost nobody can do.

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| Which sounds most like you on your best day? | Effortless and cozy · Sharp and put together · Bold and artistic · Soft and romantic |
| Pick a place whose style you've always loved | A café in Paris · A street in New York · A sunny beach town · A cozy cabin in Scandinavia |
| Walking into a store, what would you grab first? | A crisp white shirt · A statement jacket · A flowy dress or soft knit · A great pair of sneakers |
| Three looks, which excites you most? | Write three directions from their answers. If they chose "surprise me", make one slightly unexpected |

### Silhouette, only for tops, dresses or bottoms

Necklines to skip and rise moved to Stage 2b, because they decide the shortlist rather
than rank it. What is left here is the one that genuinely ranks.

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| Necklines you especially love? | Square · V-neck · Crew or scoop · No favourites (multi) |

## Stage 4, fit and sizing

| Question | Options | Shown when |
|---|---|---|
| How do you like clothes to fit? | Relaxed or oversized · True to size · Tailored and fitted · Depends on the piece | Always |
| **Core.** Size in tops? | XS · S · M · L · XL | Always |
| **Core.** Size in bottoms? | Women's: 24-25 or XS · 26-27 or S · 28-29 or M · 30-31 or L · 32+ or XL. Men's: typed, waist x inseam | Always |
| Jacket size, or neck and sleeve? | Typed, like 40R or 15.5 and 33 | Suits or dress shirts |
| Shoe size, and any width needs? | Typed | Shoes |
| What kind of heel feels best? | Flats or sneakers · Low · Mid · High | Shoes, women's |
| Between sizes, what do you do? | Size up · Size down · Stay true · Depends on brand | Optional |
| Any fit details to look for? | Women's: Petite · Tall · Plus · Maternity or nursing. Men's: Short · Tall · Big and tall · Athletic build | Multi |
| Coverage or comfort preferences? | Prefer sleeves · Longer hemlines · Nothing sheer or backless · No preferences | Multi |

## Stage 5, comfort

Ask these three together. They are short, all Core, and they are the three that most
often decide whether something actually gets worn.

| Question | Options |
|---|---|
| **Core.** Materials that irritate your skin? | Wool and scratchy knits · Synthetics like polyester · Latex or tight elastic · Comfortable in anything (multi) |
| **Core.** What washing will you actually do? | Machine wash only · Machine wash, hang dry is fine · Hand washing sometimes is okay · Dry cleaning is no problem |
| **Core.** Anything you always want, like pockets, or never wear? | Open to anything · Yes, let me type it |

| Question | Options | Shown when |
|---|---|---|
| **Core.** What budget feels comfortable for the whole cart today? | Under $100 · $100 to $250 · $250 to $500 · $500 and up | Always |
| When do you need it to arrive? | Within a few days · Within two weeks · Within a month · No rush | An event, new job or date |
| **Core.** Where would you like me to shop? | I have a store in mind · Suggest some for me | Always |
| Anything that matters when you shop? | Sustainable or ethical · On sale · Free and easy returns · Fast shipping | Multi |
| Own pieces this should go with? | Yes, let me describe them · Start fresh | Always |
| How many options in the cart? | Just my top picks · A nice handful · Plenty of options | Always |
| Anything else before I start? | Nope, I'm ready · Yes, let me add something | Always |

## Returning shoppers

Stages 3, 4 and 5 are the profile: store them and never ask again. So is most of
Stage 2b, since height, rise, neckline and fit do not change between trips. The
outerwear kit's climate and layering answers are the exception and belong to the trip.
Stages 1, 2 and 6 are the brief, asked every time.

Sizes and care sit in the profile stages but appear in the opening brief in SKILL.md,
which is not a contradiction: a first trip has no profile to read from. Ask them on a
first trip, carry them forward on every trip after, and say what you are carrying rather
than asking again.
