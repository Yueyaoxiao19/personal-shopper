# Personal shopper, simulated first trip

Run of `SKILL.md` up to the moment the skill would first put garments in front of the shopper. No browsing was done; the run stops at the point the store would open.

---

SHOPPER: hey so i need new work pants, nothing fancy, just something i can wear to the office a few days a week. can you help me pick some out

CLAUDE: Hi there, I'm so happy to help you shop today! I'll ask you some quick questions you can answer with a tap, and you can always type your own answer if none of the options fit. Whenever you feel ready, just say "that's enough" and I'll start shopping for you right away. Two quick ones to start: (1) What brings you shopping today? *an event or occasion · a new chapter like a new job or season · a specific item I have in mind · not sure yet, help me find my vibe* (2) Which section, and what should I find? *women's · men's · gender neutral*, and *clothing · activewear · shoes and socks · scarves, hats and gloves*

SHOPPER: a new chapter, just an everyday refresh. women's, and clothing.

CLAUDE: Got it, an everyday refresh. Next two: (1) A complete outfit, or just a few pieces? *a complete outfit · just a few pieces* — and if it's a few, which ones? *tops · bottoms · dresses or jumpsuits · outerwear* (2) How many pieces today? *just one · two or three · four or five · a full refresh*

SHOPPER: just a few pieces, bottoms. two or three.

CLAUDE: Two or three bottoms, noted. Now your sizes, and these are two separate questions because they don't always move together: (1) Top size? *XS · S · M · L · XL* (2) Bottom size? *24-25 or XS · 26-27 or S · 28-29 or M · 30-31 or L · 32+ or XL*

SHOPPER: L on top, 31 on the bottom.

CLAUDE: Thank you. Two more and we're nearly shopping: (1) What budget feels comfortable for the whole cart today? *under $100 · $100 to $250 · $250 to $500 · $500 and up* (2) What washing will you actually do? *nothing dry-clean-only · machine wash, hang dry is fine · hand washing sometimes is okay · dry cleaning is no problem*

SHOPPER: $100 to $250. nothing dry-clean-only.

CLAUDE: Last one from this part: where shall I shop? *I have a store in mind · suggest some for me*

SHOPPER: suggest some for me

CLAUDE: Then let's start at Uniqlo. For two or three machine-washable office trousers inside $250 it's the obvious first stop, their trousers are sized in inches so a 31 is a real number rather than a guess, and if the fit misses I'd try Everlane or Banana Republic Factory next. So the brief is: two or three pairs of office trousers, women's, L on top and 31 on the bottom, $100 to $250 for the cart, nothing dry-clean-only. Before I pull anything, four short questions about trousers specifically, two at a time. (1) How tall are you? *under 5'3" · 5'3" to 5'6" · 5'6" to 5'9" · 5'9" and up* (2) Where should they sit at the waist? *high · mid · low · no preference*

SHOPPER: 5'9". mid rise.

CLAUDE: (1) How wide through the leg? *slim or tapered · straight · wide · barrel or baggy* (2) Waist style? *zip and button · elastic or pull-on · either is fine*

SHOPPER: straight. either is fine.

CLAUDE: And the two I ask whatever the category, because colour is the cheapest filter in the shop: (1) Which colours would you actually wear? *neutrals · black · earth tones · brights · pastels* (pick as many as you like) (2) How do you feel about patterns? *solids only · subtle patterns · bold prints · anything goes*

SHOPPER: neutrals and black. solids only.

CLAUDE: Here's what I'm hearing: two or three pairs of straight-leg, mid-rise office trousers, size 31, neutrals and black, solids only, nothing dry-clean-only, $100 to $250 for the whole cart, and you're 5'9" so I'll check Regular against any Tall or inseam options on the page rather than assuming. Waist style is open, so I'll let that one go wherever the better trouser is. Anything else before I start? *nope, I'm ready · yes, let me add something*

SHOPPER: nope, ready

CLAUDE: Perfect. I'll open Uniqlo, use their own menu and filters rather than guessing a URL, and check the care label and the stated fit on each one before it survives. Then I'll put five or six in your cart with the size and colour already selected, so you see the real photos at full size and can just delete whatever misses. I won't check out, that part stays with you.
